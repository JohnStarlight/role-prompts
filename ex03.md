# Tone Variation with the Same Task

## Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview with a friendly tone:
Subject: We’d love to meet you! Interview invitation: **[Job Title]**

Hi **[Candidate Name]**,

I have some great news! Our team has finished reviewing your application for the **[Job Title]** position, and we were really impressed with your background—especially your experience with **[mention a specific skill or project from their resume]**.

We’d love to invite you to a formal interview to learn more about you and share what we’re building here at **[Company Name]**. It’s a pretty relaxed environment, so consider this a chance for us to get to know each other and see if we’re a great match.

### Interview Details
* **With:** **[Interviewer Name(s) & Title(s)]**
* **Format:** **[Video Call / In-person / Phone]**
* **Duration:** About **[30/45/60]** minutes

**Are you free during any of these times?**
* **[Option 1: Day, Date at Time]**
* **[Option 2: Day, Date at Time]**
* **[Option 3: Day, Date at Time]**

If none of those work, just let me know your availability for later this week, and we’ll make it happen. Also, feel free to reach out if you have any questions before we meet!

Looking forward to chatting,

Best,

**[Your Name]**
**[Your Title]**
**[Link to Website/LinkedIn]**

## Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview with a professional tone:
Subject: Interview Invitation: **[Job Title]** role at **[Company Name]**

Dear **[Candidate Name]**,

Thank you for your interest in the **[Job Title]** position with **[Company Name]**. After reviewing your application, we are impressed with your background and would like to invite you to an interview to discuss the role and your experience in more detail.

We would like to schedule a **[Phone/Video/In-person]** interview, which we expect will take approximately **[Duration, e.g., 45 minutes]**. You will be meeting with **[Interviewer Name(s)]**, **[Interviewer Title(s)]**.

### **Interview Details**
Please let us know if any of the following times work for your schedule:

* **Option 1:** [Date] at [Time, including Time Zone]
* **Option 2:** [Date] at [Time, including Time Zone]
* **Option 3:** [Date] at [Time, including Time Zone]

---

### **Next Steps**
1.  **Confirm a time:** Reply to this email with your preferred slot or suggest an alternative.
2.  **Preparation:** [Mention any specific requirements, e.g., "Please have a copy of your portfolio ready" or "We will send a calendar invite with the video link once confirmed."]

We look forward to speaking with you and learning more about what you can bring to our team.

Best regards,

**[Your Name]**
**[Your Title]**
**[Company Name]**

---

## Comparisson: 
It is practically the same email but with a different tone. The first one shows more warmth as if having a positive disposition towards the receiver while the second is more neutral, more professional and to the point, without any extra input.
